About
IGNUZ'26

IGNUZ'26 is the national-level technical symposium of the Department of Biomedical Engineering, KPRIET — hosted with BMESI, Medico Sapiens and IEEE EMBS KPRIET SBC. Two days of technical and non-technical events built around innovation, creativity, design thinking and the future of healthcare technology.

About KPRIET

Autonomous, affiliated to Anna University, AICTE-approved and NAAC-accredited. Part of the KPR Group, KPRIET offers 14 UG, 6 PG and MBA programmes on an AI-integrated campus built to Learn Beyond.
